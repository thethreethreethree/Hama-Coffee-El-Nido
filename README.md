Hama Coffee El Nido is a beachfront café located on C. Hama Street in Barangay Buena Suerte, El Nido, Palawan, Philippines. Known for its scenic views of Bacuit Bay and Cadlao Island, it combines artisanal coffee, brunch fare, and a relaxed seaside ambiance that attracts both travelers and digital nomads.

Atmosphere and Setting
The café’s bright, contemporary interior—accented with natural wood and plants—frames sweeping ocean views from its open terrace. Guests often describe the atmosphere as serene yet lively, ideal for a slow breakfast, mid-day coffee, or remote work session with reliable Wi-Fi.

Menu and Coffee
Hama Coffee serves a mix of Western and Filipino breakfast and brunch items, including pancakes, adobo flakes, and curry rice. Its beverage list features espresso-based drinks such as Spanish lattes, flat whites, and iced matcha. Reviewers highlight the smooth roast quality and consistent preparation of its brews.

Popularity and Experience
With ratings averaging around 4.6/5 from hundreds of online reviews, it ranks among El Nido’s top coffee spots. Visitors praise its beachside location, friendly staff, and quick service despite frequent crowds. Many note it as one of the best places in town to enjoy sunrise coffee or unwind after morning island tours.

Work and Community Vibe
Frequented by backpackers and digital professionals, the café is recognized for strong Wi-Fi, available charging outlets, and a relaxed social environment. It often doubles as a casual workspace where patrons linger over coffee while taking in the coastal panorama.
