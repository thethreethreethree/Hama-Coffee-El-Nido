Our Story

"One of the best spots in El Nido to enjoy sunrise coffee with a sea view."

A Café Born
From the Sea

Perched on C. Hama Street in Barangay Buena Suerte, Hama Coffee El Nido was built for those who believe the best mornings start with exceptional coffee and unobstructed ocean views. Raw concrete walls, warm wood slatted ceilings, and an open terrace frame a daily front-row seat to Bacuit Bay and the majestic Cadlao Island.

We serve carefully crafted espresso drinks alongside Western and Filipino brunch staples — from flaky homemade croissants to hearty adobo flakes. Whether you're a traveler refueling between island tours or a digital nomad settling in for the day, Hama is your place.
